Why Insider Trading Data Matters

When corporate executives spend millions buying their own company's stock, they usually know something the market doesn't. Academic research shows insider purchases outperform the broader market by 4–5% annually (Seyhun 1986, Lakonishok & Lee 2001). The signal is even stronger when multiple insiders buy the same stock independently.

Federal law requires every insider to disclose their trades within 2 business days via SEC Form 4 filings. Members of Congress must disclose stock trades under the STOCK Act within 45 days. Seli ingests every one of these filings within minutes and scores them for you.

Insider Trading Signals

Every trade gets a conviction score based on peer-reviewed research — who traded (C-suite executives and Congress members carry more weight), whether multiple insiders are buying the same stock (cluster buying), how much of their own holdings the trade represents, and the dollar value. Higher conviction = more markers of a historically meaningful insider trade.
